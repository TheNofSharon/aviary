## ⌨️ 2. Secret Keyboard Cheats
During any active game (Campaign, Sandbox, or Time Trial), simply type these letter sequences on your keyboard at any time to activate hidden developer cheats:

* **`BREAD`**  
  * **Effect:** Summons a generous celestial shower of fresh golden seeds across the entire canopy.
* **`MURDER`**  
  * **Effect:** Calls down holy lightning bolts that instantly strike and defeat all invading predators.
* **`DISCO`**  
  * **Effect:** Activates psychedelic disco lighting across the aviary while all flock members bob their heads in rhythm.
* **`DERP`**  
  * **Effect:** Toggles hilarious oversized googly eyes on every bird in the flock that wobble and roll with their velocity.

## 🪵 3. Environmental Secrets & Hidden Interactions

* **The Camouflaged Stick Bug**:  
  * In certain levels, one of the tree branch twigs is actually an undercover Stick Bug! Look closely for a branch that periodically opens two blinking cartoon eyes. Click it **twice** to reveal it with an alarmed `"SQUAWK!"`, causing it to drop a rare **Golden Pinecone** or **50-feather** bonus!
* **Cloud Rain Dance**:  
  * Click any drifting cloud in the sky **4 times** in succession to trigger an environmental Rain Dance, hydrating the aviary with gentle raindrops.
* **Sugar Glider Backflip**:  
  * Click the airborne **Sugar Glider** to trigger an acrobatic 360° backflip with a sparkle burst and `"🐿️ SQUEAK! ✨"`.
* **Aviary Companion Petting**:  
  * In **My Aviary** sandbox mode, clicking any individual flock member strokes their feathers with tender chirps and floating pink hearts (`♥ <Name> ♥`).
* **Clara the Aviary Keeper**:  
  * In the Emporium, clicking Clara or her speech bubble cycles through her witty commentary, keeper trivia, and hidden lore.
